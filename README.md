## 🔬 By day — computational pathology

Predicting cancer outcomes from whole-slide images: foundation models, multiple-instance learning, and multi-center survival analysis.

- [**PATH-ORACLE-INFERENCE**](https://github.com/ozkilim/PATH-ORACLE-INFERENCE) — weights, cutoffs & inference for our LUAD progression models (TITAN embeddings ± a 46-gene RNA signature → risk)
- [**SlideCrush**](https://github.com/ozkilim/SlideCrush) — patch & embed WSIs with every histopathology ViT foundation model in one package
- [**onco_run**](https://github.com/ozkilim/onco_run) — model-agnostic WSI inference runner in a single Docker image; collaborators just `./run.sh`
- [**KAIROS**](https://github.com/ozkilim/hgsoc-consortium) & [**PRELUDE**](https://github.com/ozkilim/prelude-consortium) — international consortia: platinum response in ovarian cancer, and early-stage lung adenocarcinoma relapse
